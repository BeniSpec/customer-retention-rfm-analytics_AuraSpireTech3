# Business question

## Who is asking
The marketing team of an online retailer.

## The question
"We are acquiring customers, but we don't know whether they return. Which customers should we focus on retaining?"

## Decision this supports
- Where to spend the retention budget (emails, offers, loyalty).
- Which customer groups need a different message.
- Whether the first purchase or the first 2-3 months is where most customers are lost.

## What a good answer looks like
- A clear retention rate by cohort, with a stated definition.
- Customer segments that marketing can recognise and act on.
- A short list of recommendations, in priority order, each tied to evidence.

## Out of scope
- Predicting future sales or building a churn model.
- Product-level analysis (covered in Project 1).
